## 2. Description of Service

Drive Cleaner is a Google Workspace add-on and web application designed to help users analyse storage utilization, detect duplicate and stale files, and organize Google Drive storage.

Key features include:
* **Storage Telemetry & Quota Insights**: Real-time breakdown of storage consumption across Google Drive.
* **Smart Scan & Deduplication**: Metadata inspection to identify exact duplicates, old versions, and large attachments.
* **ROT & Clutter Analysis**: Categorization of Redundant, Obsolete, and Trivial files.
* **Safe Trash Operations**: Soft-deletion to Google Drive Trash with explicit user confirmation and recovery capabilities.
* **Resumable Batch Cleanup**: Incremental processing designed to respect Google Apps Script execution quotas without timing out.
