## 2. Detailed Description (Google Workspace Marketplace Storefront)

```markdown
**Reclaim Google Drive Storage, Eliminate Redundant Clutter, and Audit Sharing Security.**

Drive Cleaner is the next-generation workspace storage management and digital privacy hub engineered specifically for Google Drive™ and Google Workspace™. Powered by a 2026 Spatial Glassmorphism interface, Drive Cleaner analyzes your storage consumption, reveals hidden storage hogs, pinpoints duplicate and decaying files, audits external collaborator permissions, and helps you safely clean gigabytes of wasted storage in minutes.

---

### 🌟 Key Capabilities & 5 Workspace Hubs

#### 1. 🧹 Cleanup Hub: Comprehensive Space Recovery
* **Smart Scan Engine**: High-velocity metadata analysis discovering reclaimable storage across large files, ancient files, duplicates, temp files, and empty folders.
* **Large Files Explorer**: Identify massive videos, archives, disk images, and spreadsheets consuming valuable storage quota.
* **Intelligent Duplicate Finder**: Group files by MD5 checksum and size to eliminate exact duplicate copies safely.
* **Old & Stale Files**: Uncover files untouched for 1, 2, or 3+ years that are candidates for archiving.
* **Empty Items & Temp Clutter**: Instant 1-click discovery of 0-byte ghost files, empty folders, and orphaned cache artifacts.

#### 2. 🛡️ Security & Privacy Hub
* **External Collaborator Auditor**: Audit which files are shared with external domains, public "Anyone with the link" permissions, or non-whitelisted collaborators.
* **Sensitive File Scanner**: Flag financial, contractual, and confidential documents accessible beyond intended teams.
* **Sharing Governance**: Revoke or quarantine risky public links directly from your security dashboard.

#### 3. 🗂️ Organisation Hub
* **Interactive Kanban Board**: Drag and drop files across custom categorization lanes (Keep, Review, Archive, Trash) to systematically process large backlogs.
* **Smart Reorganizer**: Move unorganized root files into structured subfolders automatically.
* **Google Workspace File Auditor**: Segment native Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Forms from binary attachments.

#### 4. ⚙️ Operations & Automation Hub
* **Trash Governance**: Inspect Google Drive Trash, assess age, and safely purge with granular multi-file selection.
* **24/7 Automated Triggers**: Schedule periodic background maintenance (e.g. automatic monthly trash clearing or old temp cleanup).
* **Audit History**: Complete chronologically logged history of all cleanup and restoration actions.

#### 5. 🌱 Impact & Cloud Carbon Hub
* **Daily Cleanup Streaks**: Gamified storage hygiene tracking daily files cleaned and storage reclaimed.
* **Cloud Carbon Telemetry**: Measure your digital carbon footprint reduction and energy savings by reducing cloud data bloat.

---

### 🔒 Enterprise Safety & Privacy Guarantees

* **30-Day Safe Deletion Guarantee**: Items are NEVER immediately erased. Cleanup actions move items to **Google Drive Trash** (`trashed: true`), where items remain recoverable for 30 days under standard Google Drive retention policies.
* **48-Hour Safety Vault**: Our persistent server-side Safety Vault snapshots every cleanup batch, providing a persistent 1-click Undo banner across browser sessions.
* **Metadata-Only Processing**: Drive Cleaner only inspects file attributes (names, sizes, dates, hashes). **We NEVER read, parse, copy, or download the contents of your private documents.**
* **Zero External Servers**: Runs 100% serverlessly on Google Apps Script and within your authenticated Google session. Your data never leaves Google's infrastructure.
* **No AI Model Training & No Data Selling**: Compliant with Google API Services User Data Policy Limited Use requirements.
```
